# History

This document records the work on the revival in the order it was done,
with the details and sources of each change. It is a log: statements such
as "left open" describe the state at that time. The current state is
described in [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md) and
[IMPLEMENTATION.md](IMPLEMENTATION.md).

## Starting point (28 October 2026)

The work began with the January 2013 client booting in Ruffle, logging in,
standing in 奥拉广场 and walking into six original scenes. Almost everything
else was missing. The plan was to secure every file of 2013 or earlier on the
live CDN first, because the operator could replace or remove them at any
time, and then to rebuild the missing systems one by one.

One rule decided every file:

1. An original from the January 2013 release, or an older unchanged one, is
   used as it is.
2. A lost module is rebuilt in ActionScript 3 against the surviving 2013
   interfaces and marked REBUILT in its source and metadata.
3. A later copy is used only for art or data whose structure matches, or as
   the base of a rebuild, and each such use is listed.

### The archive

Every file the live server (`aola.100bt.com/play/`) still held from
2010–2013 was saved: 18,368 files, 1.7 GB, checked against the server's
sizes (377 from 2010, 4,258 from 2011, 7,854 from 2012, 5,879 from 2013).
Names were gathered from captured manifests, configs, Wayback URLs,
decompiled code, strings inside every SWF, the 2013 material ids and
numbered patterns (130,892 paths tried), and each was dated by its upload
date on the CDN. [ARCHIVE.md](ARCHIVE.md) describes the archive. The upload
dates also re-judged the files then in use: 587 files kept as later copies
turned out to be originals.

### What survived of the client

Of the 293 services the January 2013 client defines
(`mmo.interfaces.ServiceConfig`), 122 had an original of the era, 155
survived only as later rebuilds (which call classes the 2013 libraries do not
have) and 16 were gone. The surviving libraries name 682 server commands in
74 extensions; the server half of every exchange was lost.

| Area | 2013 services | Era originals | Later copy only | Gone |
| --- | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| 亚比 and battles | 57 | 25 | 32 | 0 |
| Interface: toolbar, chat, map, friends, mail | 33 | 2 | 25 | 6 |
| Home, garden, zoo, robot | 20 | 8 | 12 | 0 |
| Shops, catalogs, inventory | 36 | 20 | 16 | 0 |
| Tasks and NPC talk | 12 | 4 | 8 | 0 |
| Activities, mini games, other | 135 | 63 | 62 | 10 |

The milestones that followed, each ending in something a player can do:
town (log in, walk, enter scenes), living scenes (NPCs, the control bar,
chat), shop and wardrobe, the newbie quest, battles and catching, the home,
the world map, then the long tail of activities, mini games and instances.
The sections below follow that work.

### Early fixes (October 2026)

Dead clicks on stand-ins show 这个功能还在修复中. Gathering works in the 27
scenes that have gathering points (the original GatherService, a rebuilt
beam effect, the server's 14_14 and 14_11). The play-time limit
(antiaddiction) is rebuilt as never used up. The upside-down scenes (海文3,
海文4) and the later engine helpers that re-uploaded scenes call are rebuilt.

### The 2012 story quests

After the newbie quest the task panel lists the story quests of 2011–12 under 可接, and 前往 takes the player where each begins. They run their own scripts (`taskext/task{n}`), with the NPC conversations and definitions of the later client, because the 2013 ones are lost:

- `tools/gen_tasks.py` takes from the later `task/taskdefines` (24 January 2013 build, the copy in `game/`), `task/tasknpc` and config `tconfig` the quests whose script was made by the January 2013 release (its id is at most that of the newest era script), whose conversations survive for at least half their steps and whose scripts are all in `game/`, with every quest before them. The conversations are in two places: the later `task/tasknpc` keeps those of the older quests, and the scripts that survive only as their 2015–2019 re-uploads keep their own (a `Task<id>NpcPanelConfig` class the script gives to the NPC service when it loads; tasks 2298 and 2314 name theirs through their config's `TASK_NPC_PANEL_DATA`). `gen_tasks.py` reads both (`script_talks`), so the NPCs offer and carry these quests before any script has loaded, as the later client did once it had loaded them. 162 quests, 1,150 steps, every story quest the January 2013 client could have had: 辉之碎片 (2–21), 猫王子 and 沙都 (56–88), 思伍德 (160–250), 烈焰鸟 (267–346), 上古神殿 (724–1028), the 星云 arc (1034–1155), 巨人山脉 (1214–1294), 蔓藤峡谷 (1343–1427), 古老的传说 (1434–1477), 钻石岛 (1507–1549), 幽暗冥州 (1561–1641), 大漠守护者 (1711), 美食之星 (1742–1815), 多多王子 (1827–1877), 童话之星 (1891–1946), 魔术之星 (1959–1995), 海枯之星 (2010–2055), 豆豆 (2072–2138), 凯撒 (2175–2236), 神器 (2289–2347), 糖果星 (2364–2434), 冒险王 (2443–2507), 时空 (2524–2592), 骑士学院 (2620–2690), 尖叫游乐园 (2705–2773), 音乐之都 (2786–2843), 白灵圣殿 (2871–2940), 海底七夕大作战 (2997), and the guide quests after the newbie quest (1321, 3330–3338).
